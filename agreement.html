Call Recording
Consent Agreement

This page records a written, voluntary agreement between two people about recording phone calls on one device.

  • Secure & encrypted
  • Private server (Firebase)
  • Only named people can access
  • Automatic deletion

Important. This is not legal advice. Recording calls may be regulated by the Electronic Communications Act, the Data Protection Act and other laws in Ghana. Confirm the rules that apply to you first. Consent must be voluntary.

1. What will happen

  • Calls on the device named below will be recorded by a visible app.
  • Where required, callers will be told the call is being recorded.
  • Recordings are encrypted and stored on a private server.
  • Only the people named below can listen to them using a private token.
  • Either person can withdraw consent at any time, recording stops immediately.
